# Privacy Policy — Break Free

**Last updated:** July 16, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Break Free (“the App”) is a gamified habit and wellness companion designed to help you build better self-control. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your personal data.

---

## 2. Data We Collect (Local Only)
Break Free is a local-first application. 
- **Local Storage:** All information you enter in the App (including habit names, journal/reflection entries, preferences, and levelling/gamification progress) is stored **locally on your device** using platform-native storage mechanisms (such as Shared Preferences).
- **No Cloud/Server Storage:** We **do not** transmit, store, or process your habits, journal entries, or progress on any external cloud servers.
- **Third-Party Services:** The App does not contain third-party analytics, advertisements, or tracking SDKs. It is completely clean and runs entirely offline on your device.

---

## 3. Data Retention Policy
We believe that you own your data. Because all data is stored locally on your device:
- We retain your data **only as long as the App is installed** on your device.
- We do not have access to, nor do we store, any backups of your data on our systems.

---

## 4. Data Deletion (Your Rights)
You have the absolute right to delete your data at any time. You can permanently delete all data collected by Break Free using any of the following methods:
1. **In-App Reset:** Navigate to the **Settings** screen in the App and tap **"Reset All Progress"**. This will instantly and permanently erase all habits, streaks, level progress, and journal entries from your device's memory.
2. **Clear App Storage:** Go to your device’s system settings:
   - **Android:** Settings > Apps > Break Free > Storage > Tap "Clear Data" and "Clear Cache".
   - **iOS:** Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Break Free > Delete App (and reinstall).
3. **Uninstall the App:** Uninstalling the App from your device will automatically, immediately, and permanently delete all local data associated with the App.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data online, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

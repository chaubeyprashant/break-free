# 🚀 App Store Optimization (ASO) for Break Free

On the actual phone, your app's name should stay as **"Break Free"** so it doesn't get cut off on the home screen (e.g. "Break Fr..."). 

However, in the **Google Play Console**, you have 30 characters for the App Title. You should absolutely use keywords here to rank higher in searches! 

Here are some highly optimized suggestions for your Play Store listing based on your app's features.

## 🎯 Play Store Titles (Max 30 characters)
*Choose one of these for your Play Store Title. They are designed to rank for high-traffic keywords.*

1. **Break Free: App Blocker** (23/30) - *Best for targeting the massive "app blocker" keyword.*
2. **Break Free: Screen Time** (23/30) - *Best for targeting "screen time" searches.*
3. **Break Free: Focus & Habits** (26/30) - *Best if you want to emphasize habit tracking.*
4. **Break Free: Block Distractions** (30/30) - *Action-oriented.*
5. **Break Free - Habit Tracker** (25/30) - *Strong for the habit-building niche.*

## ⚡ Short Descriptions (Max 80 characters)
*This is the text right below the screenshots on the Play Store. It needs to sell the app quickly while packing in keywords.*

1. Block apps, track habits, and reduce screen time. Stay focused and break free! (78/80)
2. Your ultimate app blocker & habit tracker. Limit screen time and stay productive. (80/80)
3. Block distracting apps and adult websites. Build better habits and regain focus! (79/80)
4. Screen time manager, app blocker, and accountability partner in one simple app. (80/80)

## 🔑 Target Keywords to use in your Long Description
When writing your full app description, make sure to naturally sprinkle in these keywords so Google ranks you for them:
- App blocker
- Screen time tracker
- Block adult websites (supported browsers only; not a DNS filter or safe search)
- Habit tracker
- Productivity timer
- Accountability partner
- Dopamine detox
- Digital wellbeing
- Focus app
- Stop scrolling

## 📝 Example Long Description Snippet
> **Break Free** is your ultimate digital wellbeing companion. Whether you need a strict **app blocker** to stop doom-scrolling, a **habit tracker** to build healthy routines, or an **accountability partner** to stay on track, Break Free has you covered. Limit your **screen time**, block distracting apps (and adult websites in supported browsers), and reclaim your focus today!

---
**Developer Tip:** Don't change `android:label="Break Free"` in your `AndroidManifest.xml`. Keep the code clean! Only use these optimized titles in your **Google Play Console > Main Store Listing** page.

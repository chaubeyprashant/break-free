## Full description (formatted preview)

**Break Free: Gamified Habit Tracker & App Blocker**

Ready to quit bad habits, reduce **screen time**, and build a highly **productive daily routine**? **Break Free** is your **gamified habit tracker**, **app blocker**, and **digital detox coach** designed to help you overcome distractions, track what matters, and grow daily streaks—without the guilt.

Whether you want to **stop doom scrolling**, **block distracting apps**, stay focused while studying, or build positive daily habits, **Break Free** gives you the structure and encouragement you need. Track your progress, earn rewards, and even if you slip, get right back on track with streak protection.

### WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH BREAK FREE

• **Habit Tracker & Routine Planner** — Add daily habits, check in on clean days, and see day streaks at a glance on your **self-improvement** journey.  
• **App Blocker & Focus Tools (Android)** — Take back focus and **reduce screen time** by interrupting apps you chose to block. Adult website filtering reads the address bar of **supported browsers only** (not a DNS or VPN filter).  
• **Streak Protection & Saver** — Use a weekly Freeze or a Streak Shield (Level 3+) so one rough day doesn’t erase your progress.  
• **Gamification, Levels & Rewards** — Earn XP and coins; unlock titles from Beginner to Hero of Break Free.  
• **Milestones & Goal Tracker** — Goals at 3, 7, 14, and 30+ days for **personal growth** and **dopamine detox** progress.  
• **Relapse & Trigger Tracking** — Log triggers and confirm before resetting a streak.  
• **Panic Button for Cravings** — 90-second breathing / urge surfing when you need help now.  
• **Mind City Builder** — Watch your city grow as you level up and stick to your **daily planner**.  
• **Mindfulness Journal & Mood Tracker** — Reflect and review on-device insights.  
• **Cloud Backup** — Optional signed-in backup so reinstall doesn’t wipe progress.  
• **Accountability Companion (Premium)** — Short alerts for a trusted person — not full browsing history.

### WHO IT’S FOR

**Break Free** is a **productivity app** and habit coach for:
• People quitting addictions, stopping scrolling, or breaking bad habits.  
• Students and professionals needing a **focus timer**, **study app**, or **app blocker**.  
• Anyone wanting a **gamified routine planner**, **ADHD organizer**, or **daily tracker** for structure (not medical treatment).  
• Anyone building habits, **self-care**, and **personal growth**.

### DISCLAIMER
Break Free supports self-improvement, productivity, and mindfulness. It is not a medical or mental health treatment and does not diagnose or treat ADHD or addiction.

### ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Break Free uses AccessibilityService only to detect blocked apps you selected and to read the address bar of supported browsers for category / custom-domain matching. Processing is on-device; addresses are not stored or sold. Companion sync (if enabled) sends short summaries only.
